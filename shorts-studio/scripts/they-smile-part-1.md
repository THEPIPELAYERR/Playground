# They Smile: Part 1 (tester Short)

Original horror series told through text messages. The tone is extreme-outbreak horror: a plague that leaves people grinning and gleefully cruel. Everything here is original; no names, characters, symbols or art from any existing comic. Gore stays implied ("blood on the glass"), never shown, to keep it monetizable.

**Format:** a fake phone chat filling the screen, synthesized sound design, 47 s.
**Series hook:** each part ends on a cliffhanger, and the next part picks up from it.

## Hook (0–3 s)
Black screen, two impact hits:
> IF THEY SMILE AT YOU
> **IT'S ALREADY TOO LATE.**
